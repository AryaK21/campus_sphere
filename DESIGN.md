# Design Philosophy

CampusSphere is crafted for modern college students, faculty, and administrative staff:
- **Clean & Professional**: Clear information hierarchy without sci-fi, cyberpunk, or AI jargon.
- **Dual Light & Dark Themes**: High-contrast, easy-on-the-eyes color scheme with seamless toggling.
- **Accessible & Scan-friendly**: Organized into clean cards, intuitive filter dropdowns, and distinct badges.
