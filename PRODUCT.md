# Product Context: CampusSphere

## Vision & Purpose
CampusSphere is the unified academic portal engineered for Pimpri Chinchwad College of Engineering (PCCOE). It bridges lecture resources, interactive visualizers, peer-to-peer discussions, campus notices, placement drives, and administrative control.

## Target User Roles
1. **Student (@pccoer.in)**:
   - Downloads lecture notes, solved question papers, and lab manuals.
   - Practices with interactive visualizers (Sorting, CPU Scheduling, Logic Gates, Subnetting).
   - Participates in discussions and learns from senior interview experiences.
   - Tracks corporate placement drives and checks CGPA eligibility.

2. **Faculty / Teacher (@pccoer.in)**:
   - Uploads syllabus-aligned study materials and assignments.
   - Publishes departmental notices and exam circulars.
   - Endorses verified solutions in academic discussions.

3. **Administrator (@pccoer.in)**:
   - Monitors platform analytics (total users, downloads, active drives).
   - Manages user accounts and provisions roles (Student, Faculty, Admin).
   - Moderates content and campus circulars.

## Design Tenets
- **Simple, Normal English**: No AI/sci-fi gimmickry.
- **Strict Institutional Access**: Restricted to `@pccoer.in` Google accounts with 1-click demo evaluation accounts.
- **Production-Ready**: Supabase PostgreSQL database + Vercel deployment.
